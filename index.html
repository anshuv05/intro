04 — Research

Selected studies.

Two academic projects where the modelling had something real to say — for investors, and for policymakers.

Panel data · 200 NSE firms · 2018–2022

ESG Ratings & Investment Decision-Making in the Indian Market

  • Constructed a five-year panel of 200 NSE-listed firms; regression analysis of ESG's financial materiality across sectors.
  • Governance emerged as the most reliable ESG pillar influencing ROA — a market trust anchor in India.
  • Measured SEBI's BRSR framework effect: a marked 83% increase in ESG's impact on firm performance post-implementation.
  • FIIs showed roughly twice the responsiveness to ESG signals versus domestic institutional investors.
Governance — strongest pillar FIIs ≈ 2× responsiveness Sector-level materiality
ARIMA · Time-series forecasting

Unveiling the Scars of Conflict: A Data-Driven Look at Gaza's Economic Future

  • Analysed the conflict's impact on Gaza's GDP using historical data, advanced visualisation and ARIMA time-series modelling.
  • Simulated GDP trends, forecasting a continued economic downturn and identifying key risk factors.
